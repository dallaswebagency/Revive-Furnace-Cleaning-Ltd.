Our process

How we clean your system, start to finish

Every job follows the same careful routine. We start at the register farthest from your furnace and work our way back, so nothing gets pushed into the parts we've already cleaned.

01

System check

A full system check and pre-test of your furnace.

02

Blower cleaned

Power to the furnace is shut off and the blower is removed and cleaned.

03

Access panels

Access panels are made into the ductwork.

Farthest first
04

Vacuum connected

Cleaning starts at the farthest register and works back toward the furnace.

05

Whip technology

Whips loosen and remove stubborn contaminants.

06

Supply & return

Both the supply and return sides are cleaned.

07

Final check

Your furnace is reassembled and given a final system check.
